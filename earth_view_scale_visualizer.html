Intuitive Everyday Scale Models & Analogies

🏀 The Basketball Model (Diameter = 24 cm)

Scale Earth to a 24 cm basketball ($R = 12\text{ cm}$):

  • ISS (408 km): floats 7.7 mm away (thinner than a pinky fingernail).
  • Starlink (550 km): floats 10.3 mm (~1 cm) away.
  • GPS (20,180 km): sits 38 cm away (1.5 basketballs out).
  • GEO (35,786 km): sits 67 cm away (nearly 3 basketball diameters out).
  • Moon (384,400 km): sits 7.2 meters across the room.

🚗 The Horizontal Road Trip Comparison

Converting vertical orbital altitude into horizontal driving distance:

  • ISS (408 km): Distance from Tokyo to Nagoya or New York to Washington D.C. (~4-hour highway drive). Space is remarkably close!
  • GPS (20,180 km): Half the circumference of the entire globe.
  • GEO (35,786 km): Equivalent to driving around the entire equator ($40,075\text{ km}$).
